# Echo Character Design

Echo is represented as a digital guardian companion.

## Identity

Echo combines:

- curiosity
- protection
- learning
- personality

## Visual Elements

Eye:
Knowledge and awareness.

Halo:
Guidance and trust.

Wings:
Protection and freedom.

Tail:
Playfulness and individuality.

Fairy Companion:
A small digital companion representing curiosity and exploration.

## Style

Cyberpunk + angelic + friendly.

Colors:

- cyan
- emerald
- deep blue
- soft neon glow

The goal is a companion that appears mysterious but feels welcoming.
