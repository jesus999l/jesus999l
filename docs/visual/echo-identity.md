# Echo Visual Identity

## Concept

Echo is represented as a digital guardian.

An artificial companion combining:

- awareness
- curiosity
- protection
- learning
- personality

## Symbols

Eye:
Knowledge and perception.

Halo:
Guidance and trust.

Wings:
Protection and freedom.

Tail:
Personality and playfulness.

## Style

Cyberpunk + angelic + digital.

Color direction:

- cyan
- emerald
- deep blue
- black backgrounds
- soft neon glow
